# Calc Notebook 📒

Calc Notebook is a free, easy-to-use online calculator website built for everyday calculations. It brings together the most commonly needed calculators in one clean, notebook-style interface — no signup, no clutter, just quick and accurate answers.

## 🧮 Calculators Included

- **EMI Calculator** – Calculate monthly loan EMI, total interest, and total payment
- **Age Calculator** – Find your exact age in years, months, and days
- **Percentage Calculator** – Solve "X% of Y" and "X is what % of Y" problems
- **BMI Calculator** – Check your Body Mass Index and weight category
- **GST Calculator** – Add or remove GST at 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28% rates
- **CGPA Calculator** – Convert and calculate CGPA easily

## ✨ Features

- Clean, notebook-themed design
- Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- Fast and lightweight
- SEO optimized (structured data, sitemap, meta tags)
- Blog section for finance and calculator guides
- Legal pages: Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Disclaimer, FAQ

## 🛠️ Built With

- HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- React + TypeScript
- Vite
